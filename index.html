CER 與科學建模架構

How Scientists Think: Claim, Evidence, Reasoning & Modeling

C - Claim

主張 / 我認為事情是怎樣？

提出明確的論述或解答，說明你的初步觀察想法。

Prompt 範例：

"Earthquakes around Taiwan are concentrated in specific regions rather than randomly distributed."

E - Evidence

證據 / 我看到了什麼資料？

提供具體的科學數據、圖表、地圖觀測或實驗觀察結果。

Prompt 範例：

"Global earthquake distribution maps show seismic activity aligned along narrow active belts."

R - Reasoning

推理 / 為什麼證據支持主張？

運用科學原理（如板塊構造原理）連結證據與主張。

Prompt 範例：

"Plate relative motion builds stress in rocks. Sudden release of stress causes earthquakes along boundaries."

AI 協作指南與規範

AI is a Collaborator, Not a Replacement for Thinking

Collaborator Principle

當 AI 給你答案時，請反問四件事：

1
What is its claim?

它的主要主張是什麼？是否有明確解答問題？

2
What evidence does it have?

它提供了什麼數據或觀測支持這項主張？

3
Is its reasoning reasonable?

它的物理推理邏輯是否嚴謹且無矛盾？

4
Can I verify it with other data?

我能否找到其他文獻或實測資料進行驗證？

"AI can generate an answer, but understanding the answer is your responsibility."

AI Interaction Log 模版預覽

# AI Interaction Log

## Question
- Why do earthquakes occur frequently around Taiwan?

## My Initial Idea
- I think it's because Taiwan has many mountains.

## Prompt 1
- Suggest three possible explanations for Taiwan's frequent earthquakes. Do not give the direct answer.

## AI Response
- 1. Plate collision between Eurasian & Philippine Sea plates.
- 2. Active fault movement.
- 3. Subduction zone mechanics.

## My Evaluation & Verification
- AI suggested plate subduction, but we verified it by comparing earthquake depth maps using PyGMT.
